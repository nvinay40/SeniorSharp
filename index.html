Made for older eyes and hands

Text they can read

Large text, with three sizes to choose from.

Buttons they can hit

Every button is big, so a shaky finger still lands.

Nothing hidden

Four tabs at the bottom. No hidden menus, and Back is always a visible button.

Sound and vibration

Every tap gives a sound and a buzz. Both can be turned off.

Help for every game

Every game explains how to play, and the help button shows it again any time.

Dark mode

Easier on the eyes at night.

Why I built it

The brain games I found for my parents weren’t designed for older adults. So I built one myself, with big text, no ads, nothing to buy by mistake, and in their own language. Now it’s available for every parent.

— Vinay

Their privacy, in plain words

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • Their name stays on their phone. It is never sent anywhere.
  • We only count anonymous app usage, never who is using it.
  • No ads, so nothing pops up in the middle of a game.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads and nothing to buy inside the app.

Does it need internet?

Only to download it. After that, the games work without internet.

My parent isn’t good with phones. Can they use it?

Set it up for them once: pick their language and type their name. After that, one big button on the home screen starts the day’s games.

Which languages does it support?

English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, Spanish and French.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Senior Sharp is on Android phones through Google Play.
